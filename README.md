## 📌 Overview
**Sister Circle** is an open-source, privacy-first mobile companion engineered as a non-judgmental digital "best friend" for adolescent girls in low-resource and conservative African communities. 

Designed to operate in high-stigma environments, Sister Circle provides anonymous, voice-driven guidance on female health, body literacy, and personal safety—combating cultural barriers, soft blackmail, and gender-based violence (GBV).

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Privacy & Stealth Architecture
* **Zero-Trace Guest Onboarding:** No phone numbers, names, or emails required.
* **Calculator Camouflage Mode:** One-tap disguise that transforms the app interface into a functioning calculator.
* **Auto-Clear Session Logs:** Local data wipes after inactivity to protect user privacy on shared family devices.

### 2. AI Health Companion & Body Literacy
* **Multilingual Voice-to-Voice AI:** Local NLP model supporting native dialects (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Nigerian Pidgin) for low-literacy accessibility.
* **Offline Period & Ovulation Tracker:** 100% offline cycle prediction engine operating without mobile data.
* **Interactive Body & Symptom Map:** Tap-to-check body guide offering safe, low-cost home remedies and infection risk screening.
* **Empowerment Audio Stories:** Self-esteem audio clips featuring relatable African female leaders.

### 3. Interactive Coercion & Pressure Defense
* **"What Would You Do?" Scenario Simulator:** Decision-based training engine handling real-world scenarios like soft blackmail, nude requests, and peer pressure.
* **Real-Time Pressure Detector:** Rule-based analyzer providing instant coaching on emotional manipulation and boundary setting.

### 4. Personal Safety & Sister Circle Network
* **Best-Friend Live Location Sharing ("Share My Walk"):** Discreet peer-to-peer GPS tracking with automated arrival check-ins.
* **On-Device Harassment Shield:** On-device text scanner filtering abusive or explicit messages.
* **Volume-Key Silent SOS:** Stealth hardware gesture dispatching encrypted SMS location alerts without cellular data.
* **Anonymous Community Space:** Moderated peer-to-peer forum for mutual support and storytelling.

---

### 🛠️ Technology Stack
* **Frontend:** Flutter / React Native (Cross-Platform Mobile)
* **AI & NLP:** On-device compressed Speech-to-Text & Local Rule Engines
* **Data Layer:** Encrypted Local SQLite / Realm Database (Offline-First)
* **Fallback Communications:** Encrypted SMS Gateway for Offline SOS & GPS Pings
